My Full Story

Learning, Building, and Growing in IT.

Welcome to my story page. Here is a closer look at my background, aspirations, and what drives my journey in technology and software development.

01

How the Journey Began

After completing my Advanced Level studies in the Commerce stream, I reached an important point where I had to decide which direction I wanted to take for my higher education and future career. I could continue along the path I had already followed through Commerce, or I could explore something completely different. At that time, I chose Information Technology because of the growing demand and opportunities in the IT field.

My journey into programming started with C, which gave me my first real experience of writing code and understanding how programs work. From there, I gradually explored different programming languages and technologies, including C++, Java, Python, and MERN technologies, while developing my knowledge through university studies, practical work, and projects.

As I progressed through my degree at SLIIT, I discovered that my interests were becoming broader. I enjoy website development and building practical applications, but recently I have also developed a strong interest in Data Science and Analysis. Studying data handling and working with Python has been particularly interesting to me, and it has opened up another area of technology that I would like to explore further.

Today, I am interested in both web development and data science, and I am continuing to learn and build my skills in both areas as I work toward finding the path that suits me best.

02

Education & Technical Growth

I am currently pursuing a degree in Information Technology at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT) and am in my third year, second semester. My university journey has given me the opportunity to build a strong foundation in programming, databases, software development, and other areas of information technology while gaining practical experience through laboratory work and projects.

Throughout my studies, I have worked with several programming languages and technologies, including C, C++, Java, Python, and the MERN stack. I have also gained practical experience in areas such as web development, database management, SQL, networking, and data analysis. Working on different practical activities and projects has helped me understand how the concepts I learn in lectures can be applied to real-world applications.

One of the areas I have particularly enjoyed is web development, where I have worked with technologies such as React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. At the same time, my recent experience with Data Science and Analysis and Python-based data handling has developed a new interest in data science.

I see my technical growth as an ongoing journey. Rather than focusing on only one technology, I want to continue exploring different areas of IT and build practical skills that will help me become a capable and adaptable technology professional.
